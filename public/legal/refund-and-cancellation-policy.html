Last updated: October 28, 2026

Cancellation

  • You can cancel your subscription at any time.
  • After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the billing period you already paid for (the current month for Monthly, the current year for Yearly).
  • You will not be charged again after cancellation.
  • To cancel, use the cancel option in your Whop account, or email rochelledevot@gmail.com and we will cancel it for you.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically each month ($99.00 USD) or each year ($199.00 USD), plus applicable taxes, until you cancel. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Refunds

All payments are final and non-refundable, including partial periods and unused time after cancellation. If you believe you were charged in error, contact rochelledevot@gmail.com and we will review it.
