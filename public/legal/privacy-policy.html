NorthStar Locate – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Devren Consu Inc ("we", "us") operates NorthStar Locate. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and phone number.
  • Phone numbers you enter to send location requests.
  • Current location: collected only from a person who has approved a request, and only while that approval is active.
  • Payment information is handled by our payment provider, Whop. We do not store full card details.

2. How we use information

To operate the Service, send location requests, show an approved person's current location to the requester, process payments, prevent misuse, and respond to support requests.

3. Location data

  • We show current location only. We do not store location history.
  • Location is shared only after the person approves the request. They can withdraw approval at any time, and sharing stops immediately.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to run the Service (such as payment processing and hosting), or when required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data by emailing us.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no system is completely secure.

7. Age requirement

The Service is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and will delete it if we learn we have.

8. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing us.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

10. Contact
